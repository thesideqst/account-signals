What did the quarter actually do —
versus how management framed it?

Reading the filing, the call, and the news takes hours. Telling the two apart — the number versus the story told about it — is the part worth automating.

the whole product is this contrast

Two inputs, deliberately different shapes

Measured
"Operating margin COMPRESSED by 209 basis points versus last quarter."
silver_metric_context — GOOG, period ending Jun 30 2026. Computed in SQL, direction stated in words
vs
Framed
"Q2 represented another strong quarter."
silver_doc_chunks — Anat Ashkenazi, CFO, Alphabet Q2 2026 earnings call

Real data, both sides. The model narrates both, side by side — it doesn't compute the gap or explain a cause, it lets you hear where they don't match.

  measured   silver_financial_deltas, already computed. The model is told

  framed     silver_doc_chunks, what management said on the call,
